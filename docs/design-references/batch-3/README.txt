ANFA ATHLETICS — ASSETS BATCH 3

PURPOSE
This batch contains campaign, brand-atmosphere and digital-experience concept references to help art-direct the homepage. The panels were cropped from an AI-generated concept board; they are not original high-resolution campaign photographs.

CONTENTS
- campaign-concepts/: desktop/mobile hero directions, an alternate campaign and Homme/Femme/Nouveautés banner references.
- experience-concepts/: Outfit Lab and ANFA ID concept references.
- materials-concepts/: logo-on-fabric, accessories and brand-origin mood references.
- campaign-concept-board-full.jpg: the complete concept board.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
Some panels have AI-generated wording or logo-like marks baked into the image. The generated text may not match the final approved brand copy, and the generated logo is not the official ANFA logo. Use the actual logo assets from Batch 1 for the website. Keep the banner references with embedded text for visual direction only; do not place them behind HTML headings unless the text is intentionally removed.

PRODUCT ACCURACY
For actual product cards and product pages, use the catalogue-derived garment assets in Batches 1 and 2. Do not present AI-generated concept garments as final approved product photography. The supplied ANFA catalogues remain the source of truth for product designs.

USAGE
These are design references for the prototype. We recommend replacing them with separately produced, high-resolution campaign photos before launch. This batch does not modify website code. If you copy these locally, store them in a reference folder rather than making every concept image publicly served.
